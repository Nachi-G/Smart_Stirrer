# Assumptions

| ID | Assumption | Importance | Confidence | How to test |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| A-001 | Users dislike manually stirring food enough to buy a device | Critical | Low | Customer interviews |
| A-002 | Stirring requires spatially varying trajectories | Critical | Medium | Cooking experiments |
| A-003 | A camera can reliably observe food through steam | High | Low | Vision experiment |
| A-004 | 3+ DOF significantly improves stirring performance | High | Low | Mechanism comparison |
| A-005 | Device can attach to many pot geometries | High | Medium | Fit study |
| A-006 | Motors can remain sufficiently isolated from heat | Critical | Low | Thermal prototype |
| A-007 | Users tolerate cleaning the mechanism | Critical | Low | User testing |
